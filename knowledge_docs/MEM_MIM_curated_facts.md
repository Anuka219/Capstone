## MEM: Engineering and Management M.Sc.

- Degree: Master of Science in Engineering and Management.
- Duration: 3 semesters including the master thesis. An optional double degree
  path may take 4 semesters.
- Credits: 90 ECTS.
- Start: summer semester and winter semester.
- Places: 24.
- Language of instruction: English and German.
- Application deadlines listed for 2026: 15 January 2026 for summer semester
  2026 and 15 June 2026 for winter semester 2026/2027.
- Admission includes a university degree graded good, at least 2.5 or better,
  in an industrial engineering / Wirtschaftsingenieurwesen program.
- Additional admission documents include a motivation letter of maximum two
  pages, an academic or professional recommendation letter, and proof of English
  at B2 level. German at C1 level (e.g. DSH-2, TestDaF TDN 4x4, or telc C1
  Hochschule) is required ONLY for applicants who are not native German speakers
  and whose previous degree was not taught in German. C2 is NOT required, and
  native German speakers (or applicants with a German-taught degree) do not
  submit a German certificate.

## MEM Course Structure

- Semester 1: Leadership, Production Strategy, Process Management, and one
  elective from Engineering, Business, or Design.
- Semester 2: Market-oriented Product Development, Strategic Procurement,
  Value-oriented Management, Management of Emerging Technologies, and an
  Interdisciplinary Research Project.
- Semester 3: Capstone Seminar, Scientific Colloquium, and master thesis.

## MIM: Industrial Management M.Sc.

- Degree: Master of Science in Industrial Management.
- Duration: full-time, 3 semesters including thesis.
- Credits: 90 ECTS.
- Start: summer semester and winter semester.
- Places: 24.
- Language of instruction: German and English.
- MIM has an "English Track" that is COMPLETELY taught in English. For the
  English Track, NO German language certificate is required for admission — the
  only language proof needed is English (e.g. TOEFL or IELTS). German is offered
  only as an optional course for daily life, not an admission requirement. The
  standard German-taught MIM additionally needs German at C1 level, but only for
  applicants who are not native German speakers. So an international student can
  be admitted to the MIM English Track with no German certificate at all.
  English Track page:
  https://techpf.hs-pforzheim.de/master/industrial_management_english_track
- Admission: selection procedure.
- Audience: interdisciplinary master for engineers and technical
  business-administration applicants.
- Deadlines in the engineering master overview: 15 January for summer semester
  and 15 June for winter semester.

## MIM Content Themes

- Technology and Innovation Management.
- Leadership.
- Process and Data Management.
- Networked Systems and Artificial Intelligence.
- Applied projects, electives, individual specialization, and possible stay
  abroad.

## Tuition and Fees

- Hochschule Pforzheim is in Baden-Wuerttemberg, which charges tuition only to
  non-EU / non-EEA international students: 1,500 EUR per semester. EU/EEA students
  and applicants with a German Abitur pay no tuition. Second-degree students pay a
  reduced 650 EUR. Some groups are exempt (refugees, permanent residents,
  exchange/Erasmus and dual-degree students).
- All students additionally pay a semester contribution (administration + student
  services) of roughly 150-170 EUR per semester, confirmed at enrolment.
- Example: an EU/EEA student pays only the ~150-170 EUR semester contribution; a
  non-EU student pays 1,500 EUR tuition plus that semester contribution.
